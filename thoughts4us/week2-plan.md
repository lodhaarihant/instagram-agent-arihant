## The engagement round (not optional)

20 minutes a day, before 19:00 IST, by hand. Ten comments: 5 reach, 3 peers, 2 readers. Hand each post to `/ig-comment`.
Never automate this part: bots that comment or follow get the page blocked.

- **Reach (5):** big Gita, quotes and philosophy pages. Comment in the first hour, before a thread is 200 deep.
- **Peers (3):** pages your size doing the same thing.
- **Readers (2):** book, journal and spirituality accounts whose followers read.
- **The week 1 list is poetry and quotes.** Week 2 needs a Gita list. I have not guessed any handles. Browse
  #bhagavadgita, #gitaquotes, #krishnaquotes, #karmayoga and #vedanta, write down 15 you like, and send them to me.
- Skip any account that argues for or against a belief. The voice guide keeps religion as wisdom, never as argument.
- Answer every story reply in the DMs, a line each (`/ig-dm`). That is the funnel.

By hand, once this week: an "Add yours" sticker story on Wednesday ("a line that saved you"), one collab post with a peer page,
and one cross-post to the Facebook page so its 596 followers come over.

## Before it goes live: the checklist

1. **Artwork rights.** The backgrounds are other artists' work, taken from Pinterest. Get permission or a licence before
   this goes on a page you want to earn from. Some pieces carry visible signatures. This is the biggest risk in the plan.
2. **Watch one reel a day with the sound on.** I could not hear the flute, only measure it. Check that it is slow and soft enough,
   and that the Sanskrit is easy to read on a small phone. The flute speed is `MUSIC_TEMPO` in `render_krishna.py`.
3. **Proofread the verses.** The Sanskrit comes from srimadgita.com. The English is our short plain wording. Have someone who reads
   Sanskrit check the 54 verses, and compare the English with the translation you prefer.
4. **Public media URL and secrets.** The media folder is about 200 MB. Check `MEDIA_BASE_URL`, `IG_USER_ID` and `IG_ACCESS_TOKEN`
   (the token lasts 60 days). Run the workflow with dry run ticked first.
5. **Flip week 1 to week 2 cleanly.** Week 1 ends Fri 23 Oct. `autopost.py` reads every file in `schedule/`, so week 2 starts by itself
   at 05:00 on Sat 24 Oct once the media is public.
6. **Look at the preview.** Open `preview_week2.html` and scroll one full day before the first post.

## How we judge it, and what Week 3 does with the answer

Read these on Sunday 1 Nov, in this order:

1. **Shares and saves per reel.** They matter more than likes. Which verse and which theme got the most?
2. **Reel watch time.** Did people stay past the Sanskrit? If they drop at 2 seconds, the opening needs a plainer first line.
3. **Which background won.** Warm amber, cosmic, teal water or the pale watercolour. Do more of the winner.
4. **Sanskrit-first reels against the plain-words reels.** The 15:00 and 22:00 reels have no Sanskrit. If they hold viewers as well, the Sanskrit is not what keeps people watching.
5. **Follows per reach.** This is the number that says people want the page, not just one post.

Decision rules: keep any format in the top third on shares and saves. Cut any slot in the bottom third twice in a row.
Move the hero reels to the best-performing hour. If one theme clearly wins, give it two days in week 3.

## Risks

- **Takedowns:** artwork complaints. Mitigation: item 1 above.
- **Fatigue:** 20 posts a day is a lot. Mitigation: the metrics above, and cut hard in week 3.
- **Scripture accuracy:** a wrong verse loses the people who know. Mitigation: item 3 above.
- **Token expiry:** posts stop silently. Mitigation: set a reminder to renew it by 15 Nov.
