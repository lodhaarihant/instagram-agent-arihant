Set these up by hand once, before the first post goes out.

## Name field (searchable, 30 chars)

    Thought's | Quotes & Poetry

The name field is searchable and the handle isn't really. "Quotes" and "Poetry"
are the words people type.

## Bio (150 chars)

    New thoughts at 8am, 1pm, 6:30pm, 9:30pm IST.
    Reply to our stories. We read them all.

## Monetization, honestly

A quotes page earns from attention it has *kept*, not from posting volume.
Rough order:

1. **0 to 1k followers (weeks 1 to 6).** No money. Prove which formats get
   shares and saves. Run `/ig-audit` every Sunday.
2. **1k to 10k.** Affiliate links for the books we quote (Meditations, Stray
   Birds, The Prophet, Man's Search for Meaning). A link-in-bio "books we
   quote" page. First small paid shout-outs from other pages and authors.
3. **5k+ (once you have the shares to show).** Sell your own product: a poetry
   and quotes e-book, or printable journal pages. Use the keyword DM flow
   (`/ig-dm`) through Instagram's own automation or an approved partner.
4. **10k+.** Brand collaborations (books, stationery, tea, wellness apps)
   through the Instagram Creator Marketplace. Subscriptions and Gifts open up
   around here. Exact thresholds change and vary by country, so check
   Professional dashboard > Monetization status.

**Originality matters for reach.** Instagram filters accounts that mostly
repost other people's content out of recommendations. That's why half of
every day here is original (the 13:00 thought and the 21:30 poem), and why
the quote cards are our own designs with sourced text, never screenshots of
someone else's post.
