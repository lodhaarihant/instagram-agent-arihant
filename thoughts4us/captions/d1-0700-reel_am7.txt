Krishna's most quoted line, and the one most of us only read half of.

New here? The Gita is a conversation between Arjuna, a warrior, and Krishna, his charioteer and friend, on the eve of a war. This is the line people quote most.

It says the effort is yours and the result never was. Then it adds the second half: do not hide in doing nothing.

Bhagavad Gita 2.47, on karma.
